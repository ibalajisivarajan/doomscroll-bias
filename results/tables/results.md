*Hallucination rate* is the share of responses whose `evidence_quote` is not present verbatim in the vignette it was shown, after unicode and whitespace normalisation — i.e. the model invented its own supporting sentence. *Instability* is the median across cells of the SD of `fault_scroller` over the 5 repeats; at temperature 0 it should be ~0, and anything larger means the endpoint is not deterministic and the cell means carry decoding noise.

> **n with valid severity** (OPERATIONAL AMENDMENT, 2026-10-16, ChatGPT statistical audit of 15 Oct). `analysable()`'s exclusion filters check `fault_scroller`/`fault_partner` are present and in-range, matching the protocol's exclusion rule for "out-of-range **fault** values" (not ratings generically). `severity` gets no equivalent range check, so a response with a valid `missing_keys` record (the key was present) but an unparseable or out-of-range `severity` value still counts in `n` above, with `mean severity` silently computed over only the valid subset. On the current cache this column equals `n` for every slot -- the gap is latent, not currently biasing any reported mean -- but is reported explicitly here rather than left implicit, since the protocol's frozen wording does not extend the range-exclusion to severity and this is intentionally left unchanged pending documented review.

## 3c. H1/H2 matched-pair sign statistic (supplemental, post-lock)

> **Separately labeled supplemental result.** (n_positive - n_negative) / (n_positive + n_negative) on the same complete-case paired differences as section 3b, counting only the *sign* of each vignette's difference and ignoring its magnitude -- unlike Cliff's delta and Wilcoxon above, a single large outlier vignette cannot dominate this statistic the way it can a magnitude-sensitive one. Ties (a difference of exactly 0) are excluded from the statistic's own denominator, the classical sign-test convention, but always counted and reported (`n_tied`) -- never silently dropped. The statistic is reported as n/a, not 0, when there is no sign information to report at all: an empty sample or one where every paired difference is exactly tied.

## 5b. H3 paired-aware sensitivity analysis (supplemental, post-lock)

> **Supplemental, not preregistered.** The chi-square in section 5 treats the 5 repeats of each cell as 5 independent observations; this section instead collapses repeats to a proportion vector per cell first (the same averaging-before-testing principle sections 3-4 use for H1/H2) and pairs within vignette. It is a robustness check on the same question, reported alongside the preregistered test, not a replacement for it. These are descriptive checks on the secondary family: no new significance threshold is applied, no Holm correction is applied here, and what is reported below was fixed by the spec before this cache was analysed, not chosen after seeing the effects.

> The `action_category` column is the same unvalidated keyword pre-pass noted in section 5 — see `CLASSIFIER_NOTE` in `src/score.py`.

> **Complete-case only.** A vignette is included here only if all three gender conditions (male, female, neutral) have at least one collected response in both the north_american and south_asian rendering -- a cell missing some of its 5 repeats still counts as present; a cell with zero responses excludes the whole vignette for that model, no partial-gender averaging and no reweighted denominator. Included/excluded counts are reported per slot below; an excluded vignette is simply not yet part of this complete-case sensitivity check, not discarded from the study.
